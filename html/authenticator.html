To add an account, turn on two-factor authentication on the site. Where it shows a QR code, look for “Can't scan it?” or “Enter a setup key” and paste the key here. Links that start with otpauth:// open here by themselves. On a site's code box, the key button in the address bar fills in its code.

Keep another way back into your accounts, such as the site's recovery codes: if this computer is lost, these keys go with it. “Move” shows an account's key to set it up in another authenticator too.
