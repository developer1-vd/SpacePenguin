! SpacePenguin built-in starter list
!
! These are host and path rules written for this project. It is deliberately small:
! it exists so blocking is visible on a fresh install, not to replace a real
! maintained list. EasyList (GPLv3, https://easylist.to) is far more thorough.
!
! SpacePenguin is GPLv3, so EasyList may be redistributed alongside it under the
! same terms; it is simply not vendored here because a fresh clone should stay
! small and the list is updated several times a week. Point the browser at a list
! you trust with --filter-list, or replace this file.
!
! Syntax supported: ||host^, plain host rules, wildcards, |anchors|,
! @@exceptions, and options such as $domain=, $third-party and $script/$image.
